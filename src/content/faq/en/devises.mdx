question: "Does Mr Wallet support multiple currencies, including CFA franc?"
answer: "Yes. Mr Wallet natively supports multiple currencies, including CFA franc (XOF), euro and US dollar. Each currency has its own precision: EUR is handled in cents, XOF has no subunit and is never divided by 100. Exchange rates are entered manually — there's no call to a paid conversion API."
order: 2
---

Every account has its own currency. A single budget can combine XOF and EUR accounts: amounts are
converted for display using a rate you enter yourself, never recalculated backward on the stored
amounts. You stay in full control of the rate used.
