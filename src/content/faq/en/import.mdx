---
question: "Can I import my bank statements from a file?"
answer: "Yes, Mr Wallet accepts CSV, TSV, XLSX and XLS files up to 10 MB. Encoding, delimiter and date format are detected automatically, and every row is checked against your existing transactions to prevent duplicates before anything is saved. Nothing is written until you confirm the preview."
order: 3
---

The mapping from your file's columns to a transaction's fields (date, amount, label, category) can
be saved as a reusable template, so your second import from the same bank is instant. No direct
connection to your bank is required or offered: you export the file from your own banking portal.
