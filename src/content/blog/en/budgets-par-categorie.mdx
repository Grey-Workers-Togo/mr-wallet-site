A Mr Wallet budget is a cap you set per category and period — groceries, transport, entertainment
— which triggers an alert before it's exceeded, rather than a bare summary you notice after the
month is already over.

## Why an alert before, not a summary after

Most budgeting apps show you what you spent once the month is done: too late to adjust anything.
A budget with an alert warns you while you can still decide — cut back on a category, delay a
purchase.

## A budget per category, not one global number

Setting a single global monthly cap hides imbalances: you can be on target overall while blowing
through the transport budget and barely touching the entertainment one. A per-category cap makes
that imbalance visible immediately.

## It combines with recurring transactions

Recurring transactions (rent, subscriptions, salary) are automatically posted to their category
each period, with no re-entry: your budget stays current without manual tracking effort on fixed
expenses.
